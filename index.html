USDA FSA Land Buying Playbook

Direct Federal Government Real Estate Purchases

​

Check Official USDA Resale Portal

The USDA Farm Service Agency acquires land through farm loan defaults. Filter state to Texas on official portals to see live maps and listings.

​

Unimproved Vacant Land Filter

Focus strictly on tracts with zero houses or barns, direct public county road access, and clear boundaries on county GIS.

​

Apply the 1/3 Market Value Formula

Analyze recent sales comps for adjacent parcels on CAD. Set your maximum sealed offer bid at 33% (one-third) of neighboring market values.

​

Submit Written Form FSA-2571

There are no live auctions or real estate agent fees. Complete written sealed offer Form FSA-2571 / RD 1955-45 and deliver directly to the designated USDA Farm Loan Manager.
